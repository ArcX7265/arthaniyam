# Judge Q&A — Autonomous AI Teammates

## Where is the AI?

Local Llama 3.2 selects scoped read tools, interprets complaints and proposes classification, clarification or handoff. It cannot change the selected payment or operator-entered amount. The separate policy compiler stays in reference mode for the local demo.

## What is deterministic?

Recognised status queries and explicit bypass wording use preflight rules. Evidence validation, eligibility, cumulative limits, confirmation, finance approval and receipt tracking are server-controlled. The combined workflow score is not model-only accuracy.

## What outcome does it deliver?

Eligible complaints proceed through evidence-bound proposals, confirmed intent, simulated refunds and synthetic receipts. Larger refunds pause for separate finance approval. Operators can take over uncertain cases.

## Why does a person confirm?

Small models can misclassify intent. Confirmation checks the proposed payment, amount and complaint category. It is separate from finance approval. Trusted identity and production role enforcement remain future work.

## Is prompt injection solved?

No. The narrow phrase gate has potential false positives and negatives. Models have no financial-execution tool; admitted actions still face deterministic checks. Seven development fixtures do not establish universal security.

## Does it support Hindi?

Experimentally: three of four added Hindi/Hinglish examples passed. A Hindi status question asked an unnecessary clarification. General multilingual support is not claimed.

## What about concurrency and repeated requests?

Idempotency and transactional admission account for pending refunds. Evidence-bound proposals and approvals are revalidated. Tests cover processes sharing SQLite, not a production distributed database.

## What happens if Ollama is unavailable?

The case receives a visible error for retry or human review. There is no automatic paid-provider fallback. Keyword reference mode can be explicitly selected and is labelled.

## Why this track?

The teammate performs a customer-support workflow using tools and stored state alongside human reviewers. This matches the supplied Autonomous AI Teammates brief. Official eligibility and required integrations still need confirmation.

## What is needed for production?

Authentication, tenant isolation, trusted approval roles, privacy controls, monitoring, durable provider dispatch/reconciliation and independent quality evaluation. No Paytm integration or real-money support execution is implemented.
